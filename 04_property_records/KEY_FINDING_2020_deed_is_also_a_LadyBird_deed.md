## Why this is powerful for Brandi's defense

1. **Rick's own title came through a Lady Bird deed — an attorney-drafted one.** William F. Melillo (the prior owner, evidently Rick's father) kept a life estate and named Rick as remainderman. When William died, the remainder vested in Rick automatically, outside probate. That is exactly the mechanism Brandi says Rick then used for her.

2. **Brandi's September 13, 2025 deed is almost word-for-word this same instrument.** Compare `05_deed/Quit_Deed_text_as_emailed_2025-11-13.md`: "for A LIFE ESTATE, without any liability for waste, and with full power and authority in said life tenant to sell, convey, mortgage, lease… without joinder of the remainderman… and upon death Richard W. Melillo, the remainder, if any, to Brandi L Rodgers." **Rick copied the deed already sitting in his own chain of title.** This rebuts any suggestion that the concept was foreign to him, that the form is exotic or homemade, or that Brandi invented an unusual instrument — she used the very deed that had conveyed the house to Rick five years earlier.

3. **It independently confirms the property is homestead** ("THIS IS THE HOMESTEAD PROPERTY OF THE GRANTOR") — supporting the homestead defense that the PR has no right to possess or administer it.

4. **It confirms Rick owned the property outright by 2025** and had full power to convey it: William reserved the power "to sell, convey… without joinder of the remainderman," and on William's death Rick took the remainder in fee — so Rick was free to make his own enhanced life estate deed to Brandi.

5. **Investigative lead:** The Diamond Law Firm (Sandra Fascell Diamond) prepared the 2020 deed. If Rick returned to the same firm/template — or if that firm has any file, notes, or the drafting history of a 2025 deed — that could corroborate authenticity. Worth a call (with counsel).
