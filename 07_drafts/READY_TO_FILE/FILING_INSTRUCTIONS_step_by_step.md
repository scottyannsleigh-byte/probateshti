## After it's filed — getting it decided (this part matters!)

A filed motion doesn't decide itself; it needs a hearing or ruling:
1. Go to **jud6.org** → "Judges" / "Judicial Practice Preferences" → find **Circuit Civil, Section 11** (Judge Amy M. Williams). The page lists exactly how that division sets motions (many Sixth Circuit civil divisions use an online scheduling system or have you email/call the **Judicial Assistant**).
2. Call the JA (number on that page; or main line (727) 464-7000 and ask for "Judicial Assistant for Circuit Civil Section 11"). Say: *"I'm the defendant in 26-002613-CI. I filed a Verified Motion to Set Aside Clerk's Default. How does Judge Williams's division set motions for hearing?"* Then do exactly what they say.
3. If the plaintiff files a **"Motion for Default Final Judgment"** before your hearing: you file a short response noting your pending verified motion to vacate and requesting it be heard first — and call the JA again.

## Help that is NOT "hiring a lawyer" (real options that say yes)

- **Pinellas Clerk Self-Help Center** — mypinellasclerk.gov/Self-Help — low-cost (~$45) 30-minute appointment with an attorney to review THIS motion before filing. They exist for exactly this. Book online; St. Pete and Clearwater locations.
- **Community Law Program free clinics** — lawprogram.org, (727) 582-7402 — even when they can't take a case, the advice clinic will review a drafted motion.
- **Gulfcoast Legal Services** — keep the online application in anyway (gulfcoastlegal.org/legal-help): "clerk default entered on my homestead, ejectment, motion to vacate drafted" is their priority language. A rejection for full representation is not a rejection for advice.
- **Florida Free Legal Answers** — florida.freelegalanswers.org — post a specific question (e.g., "is my verified motion to set aside default sufficient?") and a volunteer attorney answers online, free.
- **Judge's staff can't give legal advice but the CLERK can tell you procedure** — what document types to pick, whether your filing landed, what's on the docket: (727) 464-7000.
